        Thank you for trying my webpage!

        This started out as a project: Star Gazer, a model that learns to spot planets in the starlight NASA's Kepler telescope recorded. As I worked on it, more ideas came to mind, and I kept building until it fully flourished into this star map and its game, so others could enjoy it, have fun, and learn more about Kepler and NASA's mission to find worlds around other stars.

        I'm really thankful you're enjoying it. Have fun with it, and clear skies!
